// Real World Application

Exponents in Reality

Exponents aren't just math; they are the language of the universe.

💻

Computing & Data

Gigabytes (GB) and Terabytes (TB) are measured in 210 (1024) scaling. Computer memory relies entirely on exponents.

🌍

Richter Scale

An earthquake of magnitude 6 is 101 (10 times) stronger than a magnitude 5. It's a base-10 exponential scale.

🦠

Biology & Viruses

Cell division and viral spread happen exponentially. 1 cell becomes 2 (21), then 4 (22), then 8 (23)...

💰

Finance

Compound interest grows your money exponentially over time. The formula involves (1+r)n.

// Core Definition

Anatomy of an Exponent

Breaking down the power structure.

an

Base (a): The number that is being multiplied by itself.

Exponent / Power (n): The small number at the top right that tells you how many times the base is used as a factor.


Read as: "a to the power of n" or "a raised to the n".


Example: 23 = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8

Here, 2 is the base, 3 is the exponent. It means multiply 2 by itself 3 times.


Special Case: Any non-zero number to the power of 0 is 1. (a0 = 1)
